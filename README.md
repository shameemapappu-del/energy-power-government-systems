## 📌 About the Project

This project was developed as part of my internship in the **Energy and Power sector**.

The project provides a simple and structured overview of how government systems and administrative procedures support the planning, regulation, financing and implementation of energy and power projects in India.

It connects my academic background in **Energy and Environmental Engineering** with practical knowledge of government administration and the energy sector.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The project focuses on six major objectives:

1. Understand government organisational structure and hierarchy.
2. Learn administrative procedures and protocols followed in government offices.
3. Study Acts, rules and regulations applicable to the energy and power sector.
4. Understand budgeting and financial management in government.
5. Study government schemes and programmes related to energy and power.
6. Understand inter-departmental coordination and communication.

---

## ⚡ Key Topics Covered

### Government Structure
- Ministry of Power
- Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE)
- Central Electricity Authority (CEA)
- Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC)
- State Electricity Regulatory Commissions (SERCs)
- Electricity Distribution Companies (DISCOMs)

### Administrative Procedures
- File processing
- Official correspondence
- Technical scrutiny
- Financial scrutiny
- Administrative approval
- Procurement
- Project implementation
- Monitoring and auditing

### Energy Laws and Regulations
- Electricity Act, 2003
- Energy Conservation Act, 2001
- Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022
- Electricity (Rights of Consumers) Rules
- Applicable electricity regulations and safety requirements

### Budgeting and Financial Management
- Requirement identification
- Cost estimation
- Budget proposal
- Budget allocation
- Administrative approval
- Expenditure sanction
- Procurement
- Payment and accounting
- Audit and monitoring

### Government Energy Programmes
- Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme (RDSS)
- PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana
- PM-KUSUM
- National Green Hydrogen Mission
- Green Energy Corridor
